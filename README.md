A 19th-century geopolitical wargame based on the politics and diplomacy of European Great Powers in Victorian Age. Prototype link, rulebook and synopsis are here.  
[Preview](main_map.jpeg)


## Introduction of Gameplay:
  "Velvet War" is a heavy-strategy card-driven boardgame with extensive simulation in diplomacy and colonial expansion during Victorian Age. A total of 2-6 players act as Great Powers in Europe, developing economy, expanding their territories overseas and starting a grand war with other powers if necessary.
- This game is very interesting, as it deeply test players' ability in decision making, i.e. whom to ally, whom to start a war, and how to maintain a balance among other players. The scenarios are so changeable during the game that your ally at this moment could possibly be your biggest threat in the next round.
- Another setting is the balance between military and economy. Your industry and trade income decide how many cards to draw each round; while during the war, every time you mobilize, your potential income in the future will be reduced. So here comes a dilemma: should you keep peace with those annoying neighbors, or should you mobilize a grand army to destroy them, despite an incoming revolt or revolution?
- Last, for those enthusiasts of European history, this game might be your favorite wargame. From 1840s to 1910s, plenty of historical events and characters, written on the cards, have been incorporated well into the mechanisms of this game, and many of them can even be a turning point and thus influencing the entire game.
